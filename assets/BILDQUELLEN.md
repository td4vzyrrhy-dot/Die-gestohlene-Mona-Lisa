## Ermittlerbüro

- Datei: `ermittlerbuero.png`
- Mit der integrierten Bildgenerierung (`image_gen`) für diese Anwendung erstellt.
- Verwendung: Hintergrundbild. Dekorative Szene ohne Hinweise auf die Lösung.

### Verwendeter Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: immersive full-screen background photograph for a German school detective mystery website about a stolen painting. A realistic, atmospheric Paris art-crime investigator's office at dusk, wide landscape 16:9 composition. Old walnut desk with closed case folders and a small brass desk lamp in the lower right, an evidence pinboard of indistinct photographs and red threads on the right wall, shelves with archive boxes, tall old Parisian window, warm lamplight, charcoal walls, restrained dark red and aged gold accents. Camera at standing eye level across the room, architectural interior photography, tactile wood and paper, believable mature cinematic atmosphere, detailed and elegant, not horror. Keep the left third darker and visually quiet for large cream title text to be overlaid by the website. Let the room and the illuminated desk be clearly recognizable, do not make the whole image excessively dark. No people, no weapons, no logos, no readable text, no numbers, no chemical formulas or periodic table, no clues revealing the mystery solution. Generate the background photograph only, not a screenshot or user interface.
